Questions delegates ask

Which ISO course do I need? +

Lead Implementer if you will build and run the management system. Lead Auditor if you will audit it. Manager routes for ownership without hands-on build. Tell us your role on the form and we will advise straight.

Are these courses live? +

Yes. Full consecutive days taught live online by a practitioner.

What happens if I fail the exam? +

We train you again free. Fail first time and you retake the course at no cost.

Can I spread the cost? +

Yes. 0% interest payment plans are available on all courses.

Can you certify a whole team? +

Yes. We deliver mostly to corporate teams and can schedule around yours.
