What the course covers

CIPP/E follows the official IAPP body of knowledge across three modules, taught with the working knowledge of a trainer who applies this law for real organisations.

Read the full course outline

  1. Foundations of European data protection

    EU regulatory structures, how data protection law evolved, what counts as personal data, and the responsibilities of controllers and processors.

  2. GDPR compliance

    Processing principles, consent and legitimate interests, data subject rights, international transfers, employee data, security, breach notification and accountability.

  3. Legislation and regulatory bodies

    The European regulators, their supervisory powers, enforcement procedures and penalties.
