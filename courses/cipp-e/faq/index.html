Course schedule / CIPP/E / FAQs

CIPP/E

Questions delegates ask before booking

If your question is not answered here, call us on 020 3858 9207 or send us a message. A course adviser replies the same working day.

The course itself

What are the course hours? +

9:00am to 4:00pm UK time on both days, with breaks through the day. The days are consecutive: Monday and Tuesday.

Is this course live or e-learning? +

Live. Both days are taught online by an instructor in real time, in a small cohort where your questions get answered as they come up. We do not sell recorded e-learning for this course.

Who will be teaching me? +

Every trainer here has spent a minimum of 25 years in industry and still works in privacy. You are taught by people doing the job, not reading slides about it.

Why 2 full days rather than 4 half days? +

Most of our delegates are busy professionals. Two focused days means one trip out of your diary, not a week of fragments.

What materials do I get? +

Official IAPP training materials are included in your fee, along with your exam and IAPP membership.

Booking, dates and payment

What if my plans change after I book? +

You can transfer yourself to another date of the same course, free of charge, right up to the day before the course. The link in your booking email shows you every upcoming date and moves you in a couple of clicks, without waiting on our office.

Can I spread the cost? +

Yes. We offer 0% interest payment plans on all courses. Ask when you book or call us on 020 3858 9207.

Do you run this for teams? +

Yes. We deliver mostly to corporate teams, and one enquiry can cover several colleagues. Tell us how many people when you get in touch.

How do I join on the day? +

Your joining link and course information arrive by email after you book. Log in just before 9:00am UK on day one and your trainer takes it from there.

The exam and certification

Is the exam included in the price? +

Yes. Your CIPP/E exam and your IAPP membership are both included in the course fee.

What does the exam look like? +

90 multiple-choice questions with 2.5 hours to complete them. You sit it after the course, at a time you choose.

What happens if I fail the exam? +

We train you again free of charge. If you fail your exam on the first attempt, you can retake the course with us at no cost.

Do I need any qualifications before I start? +

No formal prerequisites. Delegates range from lawyers adding privacy to their practice to professionals moving into their first data protection role.

Ready when you are

CIPP/E runs every month: 2 full days, 9:00am to 4:00pm UK, live online. See the next dates and book your place in a couple of clicks.
