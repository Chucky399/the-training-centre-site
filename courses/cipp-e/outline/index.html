CIPP/E course outline

Everything the 2 days cover

CIPP/E follows the official IAPP body of knowledge across three modules. You are taught live, 9:00am to 4:00pm UK on both days, by a trainer with at least 25 years in industry who still works in privacy.

Module 1

Foundations of European data protection

  • The EU regulatory structures and institutions behind data protection law
  • How European data protection law has evolved to today’s framework
  • What counts as personal data, and how it is classified
  • Controller and processor roles and responsibilities
  • How the GDPR and ePrivacy rules interact

Module 2

GDPR compliance

  • The elements of the GDPR and its data processing principles
  • Consent and legitimate interests as lawful bases
  • Data subject rights and how to handle them in practice
  • International data transfers
  • Employee data and workplace monitoring
  • Security of processing and breach notification
  • Accountability measures that stand up to a regulator

Module 3

Legislation and regulatory bodies

  • The European regulatory authorities and how they operate
  • Supervisory powers and enforcement procedures
  • Penalties, and what regulators actually fine organisations for

Then the exam, when you’re ready

Your CIPP/E exam is included in the course fee: 90 multiple-choice questions in 2.5 hours, booked for a time that suits you after the course. Fail first time and we train you again free. Your IAPP membership is included too.
