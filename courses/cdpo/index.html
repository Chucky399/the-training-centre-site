Questions delegates ask

How is CDPO different from CIPP/E? +

CIPP/E certifies your knowledge of European privacy law. CDPO trains you for the DPO role itself: running the function, audits, breach response and accountability. Many delegates do both. See the CIPP/E course.

Is the course live? +

Yes. Three full consecutive days taught live online. Not recorded e-learning.

What happens if I fail the exam? +

We train you again free. If you fail your exam on the first attempt, you retake the course at no cost.

Can I spread the cost? +

Yes. 0% interest payment plans are available on all courses.

Do you train whole teams? +

Yes. Most of our delivery is to corporate teams. One enquiry covers as many colleagues as you need.
