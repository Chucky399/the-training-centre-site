What delegates say

Questions delegates ask

Is AIGP worth it if I already hold CIPP/E? +

They certify different things. CIPP/E is European privacy law; AIGP is governance of AI systems. Increasingly employers want the pair.

Does AIGP cover the EU AI Act? +

Yes, extensively. The syllabus covers the regulatory landscape for AI including the EU AI Act, alongside governance frameworks, ethics and risk.

Is the course live or recorded? +

Live. Two full days online with a real instructor. No recordings sold as training.

What happens if I fail the exam? +

We train you again free. Fail first time and you retake the course at no cost.

Do you run AIGP for teams? +

Yes. We regularly run cohorts of 7 to 10 delegates and deliver to corporate teams.
