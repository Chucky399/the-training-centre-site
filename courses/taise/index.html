How is TAISE different from AIGP? +

AIGP certifies AI governance: policy, law and accountability. TAISE goes deeper into the safety and security of AI systems themselves. They complement each other.

Yes. Three full consecutive days online with a live instructor.

We train you again free. Fail on the first attempt and you retake the course at no cost.
