Our trainers

Every trainer here has spent a minimum of 25 years in industry, and every one of them still practises today. They run privacy programmes, audit management systems and advise on AI governance for real organisations, then bring that work into the classroom.

That is the difference you notice on the course. Ask a question about your organisation’s situation and you get an answer from someone who dealt with the same problem recently, not a page reference.

  • 25+ years in industry, minimum, every trainer
